who i am

hey, i'm finn

I'm Finn, an outdoor and travel creator from Utah. Most of my time goes to camping, hiking, climbing, skiing, or working on my Tacoma. That's what I share: the trips, the projects, and the stuff I enjoy along the way.

I want to show people there's more to life than the same routine every day. If someone watches one of my videos and thinks "I could do that too," and then actually goes, that's the whole point. A weekend outside or the trip they keep putting off, I want to give them a reason to go.

I like working with brands that fit naturally into that life, especially ones that open up travel, let me make a living doing what I love, and bring other people along. More than anything, I want people to be part of the adventure, not just watch it.
